### FORUM-006 - Oil-temperature sender must actually sample oil

- **Thread title:** Acceptable Engine Temps; oil temp sensor location
- **Canonical URL:** https://thefactoryfiveforum.com/showthread.php?22579-Acceptable-Engine-Temps=
- **Forum section:** Engine instrumentation
- **Relevant author(s):** Multiple Roadster builders
- **Post date(s):** 2016 and 2023
- **Accessed:** 2026-06-29
- **Vehicle generation/configuration:** SBF-powered Roadsters
- **Claim or technique:** A reducer can leave the sender tip flush/recessed and
  produce misleading readings; builders use a suitable pan bung, close adapter,
  sandwich plate, or remote-filter port where the probe is exposed to flowing oil.
- **Supporting experience:** Builders diagnosed implausibly low readings and compared
  alternate locations.
- **Contradictions or caveats:** Exact location and thread depend on Forte's oil pan,
  filter arrangement, and the supplied Speedhut sender.
- **Relationship to official instructions:** Supplements
- **Project impact:** Sender inclusion alone does not resolve installation. Confirm
  thread, immersion, protection, and serviceability with the delivered engine.
- **Related question/decision:** A-007

### FORUM-007 - Preferred SBF oil-temperature sender location

- **Thread title:** oil temp sensor location; where is the best place to install oil
  temp sensor on stock 302
- **Canonical URL:** https://thefactoryfiveforum.com/showthread.php?45863-oil-temp-sensor-location=
- **Forum section:** Engine instrumentation
- **Relevant author(s):** Jeff Kleiner, rich grsc, JJK, and other SBF builders
- **Post date(s):** 2014-2023
- **Accessed:** 2026-06-29
- **Vehicle generation/configuration:** SBF 302/331/347/351/427-powered Roadsters
- **Claim or technique:** The strongest consensus favors a dedicated sump bung with
  the probe immersed in oil. A port in a flowing filter/remote-filter circuit is a
  practical second choice. A deadheaded oil-pressure extension does not expose the
  sender to representative flow and can read inaccurately.
- **Supporting experience:** Builders report materially different readings after
  moving senders from pressure extensions to the pan, and experienced builders
  explicitly distinguish flowing/sump locations from deadheaded galleries.
- **Contradictions or caveats:** Sump readings can respond to pan airflow, and a drain-
  plug installation may leave the sender vulnerable. Final choice depends on the
  Forte pan's existing bungs and any oil-filter relocation.
- **Relationship to official instructions:** Not addressed
- **Project impact:** Prefer a protected pan-sump bung; otherwise use a flowing
  filter-circuit port. Verify sender thread and probe immersion during engine mock-up.
- **Related question/decision:** Q-011 / A-007
